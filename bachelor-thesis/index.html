Belyi's Theorem and Dessins d'Enfants

Bachelor's thesis · LMU Munich · 2026

Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Nikita Geldhauser

Download PDF

Abstract

This thesis develops the analytic, algebraic and topological background needed to understand the proof of Belyi's theorem, which characterises the complex curves that can be defined over ℚ, and introduces the related theory of dessins d'enfants. We first establish the correspondence between compact connected Riemann surfaces and smooth projective algebraic curves over ℂ. We then view Belyi maps topologically as branched coverings of ℙ1(ℂ), describe their ramification data through transitive monodromy triples, and relate Belyi curves and their Belyi functions to dessins, the combinatorial objects in Grothendieck's correspondence. After the proof of Belyi's theorem, we work through a simple example and give further illustrations.
